---
description: How to verify that leave debt is correctly carried over to the next year.
---

1. Ensure an employee has a negative leave balance from the previous year (e.g., Nina with **-2** from Workflow 2).
2. Go to **Leave Management** module.
3. Set the year to the next one (e.g., **2027**).
4. Verify that the **Annual Leave Quota** for the new year is correctly initialized (e.g., **12** days).
5. Verify that the **Used Leave** (Cuti Terpakai) has been pre-filled with the debt amount from the previous year (e.g., **2**).
6. Verify that the **Remaining Leave** (Sisa Cuti) correctly reflects the quota minus the debt (e.g., **12 - 2 = 10**).
