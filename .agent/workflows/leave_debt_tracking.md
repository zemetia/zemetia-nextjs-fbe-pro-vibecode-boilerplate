---
description: How to handle cases where an employee takes more leave than their current quota (mainly for "Cuti Bersama").
---

1. Ensure an employee has a limited leave quota (e.g., Nina with **3** days from Workflow 1).
2. Go to **Leave Management** module.
3. Apply for a leave of type **"Izin Cuti Bersama"** (Code: A.250 or similar) for **5** days.
4. Approve the request.
5. Verify that the **Remaining Leave** (Sisa Cuti) for 2026 shows **-2** or is labeled as **Debt**.
6. Verify that the system does not block the "Cuti Bersama" request even if the quota is insufficient, but instead tracks it as a debt.
