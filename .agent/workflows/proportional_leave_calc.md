---
description: How to calculate and verify proportional leave for mid-year and late-year hires.
---

1. Go to **Master Karyawan** module.
2. Register a new employee with a specific Join Date:
   - Case A (Adi): **May 2, 2025**.
   - Case B (Nina): **October 8, 2025**.
3. Go to **Leave Management** module.
4. Set the year to **2026**.
5. Locate the employee and verify the **Annual Leave Quota**:
   - Adi's quota should be **8** days (12 - 5 + 1).
   - Nina's quota should be **3** days (12 - 10 + 1).
6. Verify that the calculation follows the rule:
   - Join Date 1-15: Count join month as 1 month.
   - Join Date 16+: Join month not counted, start from next month.
